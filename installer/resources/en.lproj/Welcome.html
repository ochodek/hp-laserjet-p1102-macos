P1102 Native 1.7

USB printing for HP LaserJet Professional P1102 on Apple Silicon.

  • Native ARM64 driver, no Rosetta or original HP software required.
  • Toner and printer settings in P1102 Utility.
  • Local PDF tools for watermarks, booklets and manual two-sided printing.

Before you continue

Finish any print jobs and quit P1102 Utility. Connect one powered-on P1102 by USB for automatic printer setup. You can also install now and add the printer later.

Administrator authorization is required. No restart is normally needed.

Independent, open-source community software, developed with AI assistance (vibe-coded). Not affiliated with or endorsed by HP or Apple.
