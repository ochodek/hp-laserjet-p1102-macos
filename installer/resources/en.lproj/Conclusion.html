Ready for your first print

  1. Connect and switch on the P1102; load A4 paper.
  2. Select HP LaserJet P1102 Native in the print dialog.
  3. Open Applications > P1102 Utility and choose Refresh from USB to read toner and printer status.

If the printer is missing, open System Settings > Printers & Scanners, add the P1102, and choose Use > Select Software > HP LaserJet P1102 Native ARM64, 1.7.2. Automatic setup requires exactly one connected P1102.

For normal documents, start with FastRes 600, EconoMode off and density 3. Economy mode and low density can lose very light details.

Licence, notices, matching source and Uninstall.command are in /Library/Printers/P1102Native. A restart is not normally required.
